Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Send me an email.

What do you film?

Cars and sport. That means brands, teams, athletes and events, from quick short-form clips to longer documentary-style films.

How does a content retainer work?

We agree on a number of videos per month and a filming schedule. I handle the shooting and editing, and you get finished videos ready to post.

Where are you based?

Gothenburg, Sweden. Get in touch if your project is somewhere else and we'll work it out.

What gear and software do you use?

I shoot on the Fujifilm X-T5 and edit and colour grade everything in DaVinci Resolve.

Can you invoice our company?

Yes. I run a registered business with F-skatt and invoice through the company.

How do we get started?

Send an email to info@shotswithgeorge.com with what you have in mind and when you need it. I'll reply with ideas and a price.
